# Nawabari 0.7.0

Nawabari 0.7.0 introduces the first public slice of the canonical resource-ownership lifecycle, making claim changes concurrency-aware and enabling exact or batched atomic mutations without reconstructing the complete claim set.

## Highlights

- Defines an executable exact-resource transition matrix across `none`, `read`, `write`, and `exclusive-write`.
- Persists a monotonic `claim_set_generation` and rejects stale destructive/replacement mutations with `STALE_CLAIM_SET`.
- Requires explicit concurrency intent for complete claim-set replacement through `--if-generation` or `--force`.
- Adds an atomic multi-delta primitive for mixed exact-resource acquire/change/release operations with one locked mutation and no intermediate ownership gap.
- Exposes atomic delta mutation through `session mutate` and `resource mutate`.
- Exposes one-resource atomic transition/upsert through `session transition` and `resource transition`.
- Centralizes operation-required claim modes and their implementation-owned authority rationale without weakening existing authorization requirements.

## Compatibility

This minor release extends the public claim-management surface while preserving additive `session claim` behavior and the complete-set replacement primitive. Existing callers of `session update` / `resource update` must now provide explicit concurrency intent (`--if-generation <n>` or `--force`). No registry migration is required for repositories without a stored claim generation; they deterministically begin at generation 0.

The broader canonical ownership lifecycle remains in progress; selected-resource release, deterministic recovery projection, and final machine-contract conformance are tracked separately and are not claimed complete in this release.
